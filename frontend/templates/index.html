FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about finding care, bed reservations, and platform access.

How does Arogya Map find hospitals near me?

With your permission, Arogya Map uses your device's browser Geolocation API to find your precise coordinates, then queries OpenStreetMap Overpass servers to show all nearby hospitals and emergency trauma centers sorted by straight-line distance.

How is bed, ICU, and ventilator capacity updated?

Partner healthcare facilities log into the Arogya Map Hospital Portal to submit live capacity updates for General Beds, ICU units, and Ventilators. Facilities without a registered portal are shown as "Contact Hospital" with one-touch phone verification links.

How does the Emergency SOS priority system work?

When you trigger an Emergency SOS, you designate the urgency level: Low, Medium, High, or Critical. The dispatch queue prioritizes Critical cases first and automatically filters for hospitals equipped with functional ICU and ventilator beds.

Is Arogya Map a replacement for calling 108 or 112?

No. Arogya Map is a hospital availability finder and bed coordination platform. In any life-threatening situation, you should immediately call 112 (National Emergency) or 108 (Ambulance) so paramedics can be dispatched while you locate a receiving hospital.

Is Arogya Map free to use for patients?

Yes, 100% free. Arogya Map is built as a public health technological utility to reduce emergency delays and save lives. There are zero fees or subscriptions for patients searching for care.
