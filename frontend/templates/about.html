How Emergency Dispatch Works

A transparent, reliable 5-step pipeline that connects patients to emergency beds without friction.

  1. 1

    Incident Triage

    You select the urgency level (Low, Medium, High, or Critical) and provide a concise summary of symptoms or injuries.

  2. 2

    Intelligent Queue Placement

    The request joins the live dispatch queue, sorted strictly by triage priority first and arrival timestamp second.

  3. 3

    Dispatcher Match & Bed Allocation

    Coordinators match the patient with the closest available hospital that has confirmed General or ICU bed availability.

  4. 4

    Transactional Bed Lock

    The allocated bed is locked inside a transactional database commit so no two patients can claim the same remaining bed.

  5. 5

    En Route & Admission Confirmation

    Status transitions from Pending to Assigned to Completed once the hospital receives the patient.

What to Know Before You Rely on It

Data Freshness: Bed counts reflect the hospital's latest recorded update. When time allows, always call the hospital using the direct dial link to confirm admission readiness.
Emergency Services: Arogya Map coordinates hospital availability but does not replace official national ambulance dispatch (call 112 or 108 first).
Distance Calculations: Geospatial distances are calculated using coordinates from OpenStreetMap and may differ from road traffic conditions.
Medical Advice Disclaimer: Daily wellness suggestions and triage descriptions are informational and never substitute for licensed professional medical consultation.
